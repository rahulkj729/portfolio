01 — About

Pipelines first,
documentation always.

Four years in, the job comes down to one thing for me: making data trustworthy enough that someone can make a decision on it. Pipelines, models and SQL are how I get there.

Right now I own the data platform behind TryThat.ai — IGR records across three states, RERA registries across 21, roughly 3–4 lakh records processed automatically every day.

The part I find most interesting is GenAI on real data problems. I run local LLMs through Ollama (Llama, Qwen) with vLLM for distributed inference, combining prompt engineering, transliteration, OCR and NER to turn multilingual documents into queryable data — entirely on local infrastructure.

Underneath: Python, PySpark and Spark SQL for scale, deep SQL across SQL Server, PostgreSQL and MySQL, and the profiling, validation and documentation that keep it dependable.
